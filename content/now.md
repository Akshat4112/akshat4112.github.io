*Updated 29 October 2026 · Stuttgart, Germany*

This is a snapshot of what has my attention. The [About page](/about/) covers my longer-term background.

## Working on

I am focused on reliable agentic AI and document intelligence: grounding outputs in evidence, evaluating the complete workflow rather than a single model call, and making results traceable and reviewable. This describes my interests, not confidential employer or client work.

## Building in public

- **[PDF Tools](https://akshat4112.github.io/pdf-tools/)** is a browser application with 14 shipped tools for reading, organising and converting PDFs. Documents are processed on the device; the [public repository](https://github.com/Akshat4112/pdf-tools) documents the available tools and their limits.
- **[ImmoPilot DE](https://github.com/Akshat4112/immopilot-de)** is a client-side project for exploring German property-purchase and financing scenarios. Comparison, saved scenarios, repayment controls and reporting are implemented. Version 1 release readiness is in progress; the application is not yet released.
- **[NettoPilot DE](https://github.com/Akshat4112/nettopilot-de)** is in development as a German salary calculator and job-offer comparison tool. My current focus is the application foundation and salary-calculation work.
- **[GlyphNet](/Glyphnet/)** remains my public research project on visually deceptive domain names, with a project page, paper, code and dataset.

## Learning and writing

I am studying how to evaluate LLM systems more rigorously—especially agent reliability, grounding, failure analysis and observability. I turn some of those notes into [technical writing](/posts/). Outside work, I am improving my German and making time to cycle around Stuttgart.

This page is intentionally short. I plan to replace stale details when the focus changes, and review it at least quarterly.
