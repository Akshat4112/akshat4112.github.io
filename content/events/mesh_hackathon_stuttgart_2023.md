**Date:** April 2023
**Location:** Stuttgart, Germany

---

MESH is a Stuttgart-based innovation and entrepreneurship platform that brings together students, researchers, and industry professionals to tackle real-world challenges through intensive hackathons.

## Format

Over an intensive weekend, teams collaborated to build working AI prototypes addressing practical problems in areas ranging from healthcare and sustainability to finance and productivity. The hackathon format pushed participants to move fast — from ideation to demo-ready product within 48 hours.

## My Experience

Participating in the MESH Hackathon was a great opportunity to apply ML engineering skills under time pressure and collaborate with diverse, cross-functional teams. The event highlighted how quickly functional AI prototypes can be built when the right people and tools come together — a useful contrast to the longer development cycles of production systems.

## Highlights

- Rapid prototyping with LLMs and ML pipelines under real constraints
- Cross-disciplinary teams combining engineering, design, and domain expertise
- Direct feedback from industry mentors and judges
- Exposure to the Stuttgart startup and innovation ecosystem
