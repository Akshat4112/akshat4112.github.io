2023 · Research

GlyphNet

GlyphNet detects phishing domains that look visually similar to trusted websites. It renders domain names as images and uses an attention-based convolutional neural network to identify homoglyph attacks that text-only methods can miss.

SpeakerDiff

SpeakerDiff explores privacy-preserving speech representations with diffusion models. It generates speaker embeddings designed to retain useful speech characteristics while reducing identifiable speaker information.
