    The path so far

    A career spent
    following the hard parts.

    1. 2018
      Started building applied ML

      Machine translation and biomedical entity recognition—the beginning of my interest in language systems.

    2. 2021
      Moved into multimodal AI

      Speech, text, emotion recognition, and speaker diarization at production scale.
