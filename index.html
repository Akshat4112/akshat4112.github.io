    Engineering philosophy

    Trust is not a final check.
    It is the architecture.

    01

    Grounded

    Connect generation to evidence, domain context and explicit source material.

    02

    Constrained

    Use schemas and deterministic rules wherever ambiguity creates risk.

    03

    Evaluated

    Measure behavior continuously, including failure cases—not only happy paths.

    04

    Observable

    Make decisions traceable for engineers, reviewers and domain experts.
