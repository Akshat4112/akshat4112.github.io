    2023 · Research

    GlyphNet

    GlyphNet detects phishing domains that look visually similar to trusted websites. It renders domain names as images and uses an attention-based convolutional neural network to identify homoglyph attacks that text-only methods can miss.

    2023 · Research prototype

    SpeakerDiff

    SpeakerDiff is an exploratory prototype for studying privacy-preserving speech representations with diffusion models. It generates synthetic speaker embeddings for anonymisation experiments; establishing privacy and speech utility requires evaluation of the complete conversion pipeline.
